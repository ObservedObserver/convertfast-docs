# Examples and template previews

ConvertFast UI includes two landing page templates, `default` and `editorial`. Each contains the same seven section types, with a different visual composition. Choose the one closest to the amount of content you need, then edit the generated source in your application.

[Open the default template](/demo/default) or [open the editorial template](/demo/editorial). These previews contain sample copy, prices, company references, and testimonials. They demonstrate layout and interaction; they do not describe ConvertFast customers or paid plans.

## Try both in your project

After [initializing ConvertFast](/docs/installation), create separate routes so you can compare the templates without overwriting a page:

```sh
npx convertfast-ui@latest page create sample-default
npx convertfast-ui@latest page create sample-editorial --template editorial
```

Open both routes at the widths your visitors use. Replace the sample heading and one feature description with your real content before choosing. A template that looks balanced with short placeholder text may need different spacing for your product explanation.

## Adapt the structure

A full generated page includes pricing and social proof, but your product may not need either. Remove a pricing grid when there is no purchase decision. Remove a testimonial section when you have no approved quotes. Add an installation link when evaluating the source is a more useful next step than creating an account.

Use [individual blocks](/docs/cli#install-a-block) if the surrounding site already has a design. The [section guides](/docs/sections/hero-section) explain what each component is for and what to check before publishing. The final page should represent your product's actual capabilities and give visitors a working next step.
